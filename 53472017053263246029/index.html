04 · Remote consultation

A new dimension for online consultation.

Online consultations have traditionally been limited by a two-dimensional screen. PHAI is designed to change that.

The consultation room no longer needs four walls.

A patient can be anywhere in the world. On her own phone she captures her face, and it becomes a three-dimensional model. From there the consultation is the same whether she sits beside the surgeon or on a screen a continent away: her face is in the room, in 3D, and the possible changes take shape on it while she watches. Analyze together. Discuss together. Visualize together.

For the patient, understanding before deciding. For the clinic, a first consultation that no longer waits for a flight.

01Receive

The patient creates a 3D facial image and sends it, encrypted, to the surgeon.

Captured in moments on her own iPhone (any with Face ID) — at home, in a hotel, anywhere.

02Share

The surgeon opens the model and shares the same 3D face on screen with the patient.

Both see the same model, from the same angle, at the same moment.

03Simulate

Rotate, analyze, and perform simulations while discussing the options in real time.

Every gesture on the model is visible to the patient as it happens.

Turning an online consultation into an interactive 3D experience.
