PHAI Privacy Policy

Test version. Last updated: 17 November 2026

PHAI is an app for facial consultations. A patient takes a 3D scan of their face at home and sends it to their own surgeon. The surgeon uses PHAI on an iPad to look at the scan and to show possible changes during a consultation.

PHAI is not a medical app. PHAI helps your surgeon explain possible changes, the way a surgeon might use photos or photo-editing software. It does not decide or dictate any medical procedure, and what it shows is not a promise of results. Every decision about treatment is made by you and your surgeon. PHAI provides no health care information.

This policy explains what PHAI handles while it is being tested through Apple's TestFlight, by surgeons, their team members and patients who have agreed to take part.

Who we are. PHAI is made by Tarek Arikat. Contact: support@phai.co.

Accounts

  • Surgeons sign in with a username and password made for them by PHAI's maker.
  • Team members are people a surgeon adds to help in the clinic. A team member's account holds their name, their email address, the practice they work for, and a username PHAI makes from their first name and the clinic's name. PHAI emails them a link to choose their own password. The link works once, for 24 hours, and no password is ever sent by email. Their email address is used only for these links, is not kept in our logs, and is deleted when the surgeon removes them or they delete their account.
  • Patients join with a code from their surgeon and choose a password. The code is their username. PHAI also keeps the name the patient types when joining, so their surgeon knows who they are.
  • If you give PHAI an email address for your account, it is used only to send you a link to choose a new password if you forget yours. It is not shown to your surgeon or clinic, is not kept in our logs, and is deleted with your account.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google, they give PHAI your name and email address, and PHAI keeps them with your account. Apple lets you hide your real email address if you wish.
  • PHAI does not ask for a phone number, and uses email addresses only in the ways this page describes.
  • A patient is connected only to the surgeons whose codes they used. There is no directory of surgeons or patients.

Patients under 18

A patient under 18 can join only with a parent's or guardian's consent. Their surgeon marks the invite as being for a minor. Before the first scan, the app asks for the parent's name and agreement, and records them with the date.

What stays on your device

To make a scan, PHAI uses the camera to take a series of photos of your face, with the camera's depth and position information. The 3D model is built on the device. Scans, photos and models stay on your device unless you choose to send a scan. PHAI's own features, including Enhance with AI, run on the device.

When a patient sends a scan

  • Before the first scan, PHAI asks for your consent, and names the surgeon the scan goes to. PHAI records who agreed, when, and to which wording.
  • When you send, you choose the surgeon. The scan goes to that surgeon's practice, never to another surgeon.
  • A sent scan includes its photos with their depth and position information, the 3D model, small notes about the capture, and when it was taken and sent.
  • Your surgeon's iPad gets a notification that a scan arrived. Notifications pass through Apple and carry no name.

Scans taken in the clinic

When a surgeon scans a patient in the clinic, the surgeon confirms the patient agreed. The photos of a scan the surgeon takes stay on the surgeon's iPad and are not sent to PHAI's server. Scans taken by the surgeon's team are explained in the next section.

A surgeon's team

A surgeon can add members of their clinic team. Team members use PHAI on their own iPhone, for that surgeon's practice only.

  • What they can see. Every team member can see the practice's patient list (each patient's name and PHAI number), its booked visits, and its scans with their photos and 3D models, so they can check that a scan is usable. They never see the surgeon's plans, notes or simulations, or the dots on a face scan. The surgeon chooses, for each team member, whether they can also book and change visits, send invites, and delete what they added.
  • Scans they take. Before a scan, the team member confirms that the patient agreed, and PHAI records who confirmed it. For a patient under 18, a parent or guardian agrees as well. The scan and its photos go to the surgeon's practice on PHAI's server, and from there into the patient's folder on the surgeon's iPad, marked with who took it. PHAI then removes them from the iPhone. On the server they are kept like a scan a patient sends, and the photos are removed on the same schedule.
  • On their iPhone. Photos and 3D models a team member opens are removed from the iPhone when they sign out or are removed from the team.
  • Who did what. PHAI records what each team member does (folders, scans, visits, invites and deletions), and shows the surgeon who added each item. The surgeon also sees when each team member last used PHAI and what they last did. Messages to a patient about their visits always come in the surgeon's name.
  • Notifications. The surgeon's iPad is told when a team member adds a scan or a folder, or deletes something. These notifications pass through Apple and name the team member, never the patient.
  • The patient list. So that team members can find the right patient, PHAI's server keeps the practice's patient list, with only each patient's name and PHAI number. An entry is deleted with the patient's folder.
  • Leaving the team. The surgeon can remove a team member at any time. Their sign-ins end at once and their email address is deleted. A scan already on its way may still reach the surgeon. Their account, with their name and username, is deleted 24 hours later, and their name stays on what they added. A team member can also delete their own account in the app: it is deleted at once, with their email address, and what they added stays with the practice, with their name on it.

The surgeon's back-up

So that a surgeon's work isn't lost with an iPad, PHAI keeps a copy of it in the surgeon's practice space on PHAI's server: the 3D models, the first photo of each scan, and the surgeon's plans and notes. It comes back when the surgeon signs in on another iPad. Only that practice can read it.

The surgeon's calendar

A surgeon can let PHAI add booked consultations to a calendar on their iPad. Each event shows only "Consult", the patient's initials and their PHAI number. If that calendar is one the surgeon keeps with Google, Microsoft or Apple, that company holds the event under its own privacy policy.

Your next visit

If your clinic books a visit and tells you, PHAI shows you its date, time, place and the clinic's name.

A patient's calendar link

A patient can link their own calendar (Apple, Google or Outlook) to the consultations their surgeon booked in PHAI. Each entry shows only "Consultation", the clinic's name and the time. Anyone who has the link can see those entries, so the link is long and impossible to guess, and the patient can switch it off in PHAI at any time, which stops it at once. PHAI never reads the patient's calendars.

Emails and notifications

  • If your clinic enters your email address, PHAI uses it only to send that clinic's invite or booking, through our email provider (Resend). It is kept on the clinic's record, not in our logs, and is deleted with it.
  • Your phone may get notifications from PHAI about bookings and scan requests. They name only the clinic, never a treatment.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • PHAI's server is run by Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany (in the European Union). Files are private, encrypted on the way and when stored, and each download link works only for a few minutes.
  • PHAI's emails (a clinic's invite or booking, and links to choose a password) are sent by Resend, our email provider, in the United States.
  • A patient's scans can be seen by that patient, by the surgeons they sent them to, and by those surgeons' team members. No other surgeon, team member or patient can see them.
  • PHAI's maker runs the server and its private control page. The control page can open any record, scans included, only after a second sign-in step, and every opening of a scan is recorded.
  • PHAI stores no health information in Apple's iCloud. Earlier test versions of PHAI stored sent scans in iCloud. Those versions keep working until they expire. To have anything from them deleted, email support@phai.co.

How long photos are kept

  • The photos of a sent scan are removed from PHAI's server 3 days after the surgeon has finished working on the scan with Enhance, unless the surgeon marks the scan to keep its photos. They are never removed before the 3D model is safely stored.
  • If a sent scan is never enhanced, its photos are removed from the server after 60 days.
  • The 3D model, the first photo and the surgeon's plans stay until they are deleted.
  • During the test, photos on your own device are not removed by PHAI.

Improving PHAI

PHAI places dots on a face scan, and surgeons sometimes move them. To make PHAI better, we copy those dot positions and, when needed, the 3D scan itself, including the picture of the skin on it. These copies carry no name and are stored apart from your account. Scans of anyone under 18 are never used. The copies are used only to improve PHAI and are deleted after use, or sooner if you delete the scan or your account. An improved version is checked before it is used and comes as a fixed update; PHAI does not learn on your device.

Technical logs

PHAI keeps a technical record of what the app did, to find and fix problems. Names and email addresses are removed from it before it leaves the device. Apple also shares crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Apple handles that information.

What PHAI never does

  • PHAI does not sell your information.
  • PHAI shows no advertising and uses no outside analytics or advertising services.
  • PHAI never uses face data for marketing.

Deleting your information

  • You can delete your account inside the app.
  • Deleting a patient's account removes their scans from PHAI's server and from their surgeon's iPad the next time it connects.
  • A deleted item is hidden for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone, and then erased everywhere. The server's own safety copies age out 7 days after that.
  • Scans that stay on your device are removed when you delete them in the app, or delete the app.
  • You can also email support@phai.co to ask for anything to be deleted.

When the test ends

Test accounts and their scans are erased from the test server. Surgeons' practices that take part for real, with the patients who joined them from home, move to PHAI's live system. No copies used to improve PHAI are kept. Nothing on anyone's device is changed.

Changes

PHAI is in testing, and the way it handles information will change as it develops. When it does, this page and the date at the top are updated.
