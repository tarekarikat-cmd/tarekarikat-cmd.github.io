PHAI Privacy Policy

Test version. Last updated: 11 November 2026

PHAI is an app for facial consultations. A patient takes a 3D scan of their face at home and sends it to their own surgeon. The surgeon uses PHAI on an iPad to look at the scan and to show possible changes during a consultation.

PHAI is not a medical app. PHAI helps your surgeon explain possible changes, the way a surgeon might use photos or photo-editing software. It does not decide or dictate any medical procedure, and what it shows is not a promise of results. Every decision about treatment is made by you and your surgeon. PHAI provides no health care information.

This policy explains what PHAI handles while it is being tested through Apple's TestFlight, by surgeons and patients who have agreed to take part.

Who we are. PHAI is made by Tarek Arikat. Contact: support@phai.co.

Accounts

  • Surgeons sign in with a username and password made for them by PHAI's maker.
  • Patients join with a code from their surgeon and choose a password. The code is their username. PHAI also keeps the name the patient types when joining, so their surgeon knows who they are.
  • PHAI does not ask for an email address or a phone number to make an account.
  • A patient is connected only to the surgeons whose codes they used. There is no directory of surgeons or patients.

Patients under 18

A patient under 18 can join only with a parent's or guardian's consent. Their surgeon marks the invite as being for a minor. Before the first scan, the app asks for the parent's name and agreement, and records them with the date.

What stays on your device

To make a scan, PHAI uses the camera to take a series of photos of your face, with the camera's depth and position information. The 3D model is built on the device. Scans, photos and models stay on your device unless you choose to send a scan. PHAI's own features, including Enhance with AI, run on the device.

When a patient sends a scan

  • Before the first scan, PHAI asks for your consent, and names the surgeon the scan goes to. PHAI records who agreed, when, and to which wording.
  • When you send, you choose the surgeon. The scan goes to that surgeon's practice only, never to another surgeon.
  • A sent scan includes its photos with their depth and position information, the 3D model, small notes about the capture, and when it was taken and sent.
  • Your surgeon's iPad gets a notification that a scan arrived. Notifications pass through Apple and carry no name.

Scans taken in the clinic

When a surgeon scans a patient in the clinic, the surgeon confirms the patient agreed. The photos of a clinic scan stay on the surgeon's iPad and are not sent to PHAI's server.

The surgeon's back-up

So that a surgeon's work isn't lost with an iPad, PHAI keeps a copy of it in the surgeon's practice space on PHAI's server: the 3D models, the first photo of each scan, and the surgeon's plans and notes. It comes back when the surgeon signs in on another iPad. Only that practice can read it.

The surgeon's calendar

A surgeon can let PHAI add booked consultations to a calendar on their iPad. Each event shows only "Consult", the patient's initials and their PHAI number. If that calendar is one the surgeon keeps with Google, Microsoft or Apple, that company holds the event under its own privacy policy.

A patient's calendar link

A patient can link their own calendar (Apple, Google or Outlook) to the consultations their surgeon booked in PHAI. Each entry shows only "Consultation", the clinic's name and the time. Anyone who has the link can see those entries, so the link is long and impossible to guess, and the patient can switch it off in PHAI at any time, which stops it at once. PHAI never reads the patient's calendars.

Where it is stored and who can see it

  • PHAI's server is run by Supabase, in Frankfurt, Germany (in the European Union). Files are private, encrypted on the way and when stored, and each download link works only for a few minutes.
  • A patient's scans can be seen by that patient and by the surgeons they sent them to. No other surgeon or patient can see them.
  • PHAI's maker runs the server and its private control page. The control page can open any record, scans included, only after a second sign-in step, and every opening of a scan is recorded.
  • PHAI stores no health information in Apple's iCloud. Earlier test versions of PHAI stored sent scans in iCloud. Those versions keep working until they expire. To have anything from them deleted, email support@phai.co.

How long photos are kept

  • The photos of a sent scan are removed from PHAI's server 3 days after the surgeon has finished working on the scan with Enhance, unless the surgeon marks the scan to keep its photos. They are never removed before the 3D model is safely stored.
  • If a sent scan is never enhanced, its photos are removed from the server after 60 days.
  • The 3D model, the first photo and the surgeon's plans stay until they are deleted.
  • During the test, photos on your own device are not removed by PHAI.

Improving where PHAI places its dots

PHAI places dots on a face scan, and surgeons sometimes move them. Those corrections may be used to make PHAI's dot placement better, but only for patients who have agreed to this separately. Agreeing is never needed to use PHAI. Any improved version is checked before it is used and released as a fixed version, and PHAI does not learn on your device.

Technical logs

PHAI keeps a technical record of what the app did, to find and fix problems. Names and email addresses are removed from it before it leaves the device. Apple also shares crash reports and any feedback you send through TestFlight. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Apple handles that information.

What PHAI never does

  • PHAI does not sell your information.
  • PHAI shows no advertising and uses no outside analytics or advertising services.
  • PHAI never uses face data for marketing.

Deleting your information

  • You can delete your account inside the app.
  • Deleting a patient's account removes their scans from PHAI's server and from their surgeon's iPad the next time it connects.
  • A deleted item is hidden for 30 days, so a mistake can be undone, and then erased everywhere. The server's own safety copies age out 7 days after that.
  • Scans that stay on your device are removed when you delete them in the app, or delete the app.
  • You can also email support@phai.co to ask for anything to be deleted.

When the test ends

Test accounts and their scans are erased from the test server. Surgeons' practices that take part for real, with the patients who joined them from home, move to PHAI's live system. Dot corrections from patients who agreed to their use are kept. Nothing on anyone's device is changed.

Changes

PHAI is in testing, and the way it handles information will change as it develops. When it does, this page and the date at the top are updated.
